# User Manual

## Login
Users can log in using their credentials at `/login.php`. Super admins log in at `/super-admin/login.php`.

## Roles
- **Super Admin**: Full access to the system.
- **Agency Owner**: Manage their agency, staff, projects, and clients.
- **Manager**: Manage projects and assign tasks.
- **CRM**: Client relationship management and support.
- **Employee**: View and complete assigned tasks.
