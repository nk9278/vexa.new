# VEXA User Manual

Welcome to VEXA! Depending on your role, your dashboard provides scoped access:
- **Agency Owners:** Track high-level analytics, monitor payments, assign Managers, and connect your Google Drive.
- **Managers:** Create clients, provision projects, specify budgets, and assign your CRMs.
- **CRMs:** Create actionable tasks for employees inside manager-assigned projects, review their submissions, request revisions, and finalize workloads.
- **Employees:** Review your specific project task assignments, view manager-supplied guidelines, upload media/audio-notes, and submit work for CRM review.
