# VEXA Admin Manual

As a Super Admin, your responsibility is provisioning agencies on the SaaS platform.
- You can create, edit, suspend, and configure payment subscription packages for entire agencies at once.
- When an agency is created, you will be provided a secure, one-time temporary password. Distribute this strictly to the Agency Owner, who will be forced to change it upon first login.
- Do not edit or upload files on behalf of an agency.
